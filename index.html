Mastermind BD

Crack the secret colour code!

How to play

  1. The computer picks a secret row of coloured pegs. Colours may repeat.
  2. Build your guess from the palette, then press Submit guess.
  3. ● black = right colour in the right place. ○ white = right colour, wrong place.
  4. Crack the code before you run out of guesses.

Changing difficulty starts a fresh game.
